# Third-party notices

## Iconoir

The Mini App bottom navigation uses four SVG icon geometries from the Iconoir project:
Home Simple Door, Multiple Pages, Magic Wand, and Heart.
PULSE.DV applies its own restrained activation motion while preserving the original icon geometry.
