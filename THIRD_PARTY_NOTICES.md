## AnimateIcons

The PULSE.DV Mini App bottom navigation uses the Huge-style geometry and motion
patterns from the open-source AnimateIcons project for the following components:

- Home01Icon
- Dashboard01Icon
- SearchIcon
- HeartIcon

PULSE.DV integrates those four icons into its own navigation, active-mask and
glass-pill behavior.
