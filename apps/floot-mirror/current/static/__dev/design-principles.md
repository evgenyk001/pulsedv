Optical glass, not glassmorphism: PULSE.DV should feel like a native Apple product for high-consideration real estate, with translucent materials used only where they clarify hierarchy and interaction. The visual thread is cool coastal daylight, restrained graphite typography and one muted coral-red brand signal derived from the PULSE.DV logo. Never use saturated red expanses, neon gradients, heavy shadows or stacked frosted cards.

Primary experience is light, with a complete dark token set for Telegram/system compatibility. Pacing is generous: mobile-first, large breathing room, strong vertical rhythm, 44–52 px tap targets and carefully controlled density.

Surfaces use layered translucency: near-white canvas, lightly tinted glass surfaces, hairline white/grey borders, subtle background blur and soft directional shadow. Radius is systematic: 12 px for small controls, 18 px for cards, 24–28 px for large floating surfaces and full pills for navigation states. The bottom navigation is a floating optical-glass dock with a compact active pill, not a full-width colored bar.

Typography should feel native iOS: SF Pro system stack wherever available, tight tracking on titles, tabular numbers for prices and mortgage values, medium/semibold hierarchy rather than oversized marketing headings. Icons are thin, rounded, consistent Lucide line icons; active states may fill only the icon container/pill.

Brand red is a refined coral-crimson accent (#D94A50 family), used for one primary CTA, small active indicators, status chips and the active navigation affordance. Most interface structure is neutral. Blue is reserved for map/location information and system-style links.

Cards share one geometry and material language. Property cards prioritize imagery, title, location, price and one compact status line. Promotional banners are editorial and image-led, with copy on a quiet glass overlay. Avoid visual noise: no decorative gradients unless they simulate light through glass, no repeated badges, no excessive dividers.

Motion is subtle and physical: 160–280 ms easing, 0.98 press scale, spring-like active pill travel, soft opacity/translate transitions between routes, and reduced-motion respect. Everything should look intentional at 320–430 px widths and respect iOS/Telegram safe areas.
